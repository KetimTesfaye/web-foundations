About QuickNotes

QuickNotes is a lightweight web application designed to help you jot down ideas, tasks, and reminders without any friction. It stores your notes securely in your browser so you can access them anytime.

How to use QuickNotes

  1. Open the home page and type your note into the text area.
  2. Click the save button to store your note locally.
  3. Review, edit, or delete your notes from your dashboard anytime.

Features

  • Instant local storage persistence
  • Clean, distraction-free interface
  • Keyboard shortcuts for fast navigation
  • Dark and light mode support

Keyboard shortcuts
